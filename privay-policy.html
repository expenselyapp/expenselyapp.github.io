Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026

Overview

Expensely is a receipt management and expense tracking app. The app is designed to operate locally on your device. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Expensely does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or personal identifiers.
  • We do not upload your receipts, images, PDFs, or documents.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not provide cloud accounts or cloud sync.

Camera, Photo Library, and File Access

Expensely may request access to your camera, photo library, or files so you can scan receipts, import images, or import PDF documents. These files are processed locally on your device.

Local Storage

The app stores data locally on your device, including:

  • Receipt images and PDF files
  • Extracted OCR text
  • Expense details such as merchant, amount, date, notes, and categories
  • App preferences such as language and security settings

This data remains on your device unless you choose to export, back up, or share it.

OCR Processing

Text recognition is performed on device. Receipt images and extracted text are not sent to our servers.

Export, Backup, and Sharing

Expensely allows you to export reports, CSV files, receipt files, and backup files. These files are created only when you choose to export or share them.

Once you share exported files with another app or service, that app or service handles the data according to its own privacy practices.

Security

Expensely may use device-provided security features such as Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode protection. Password-related information is stored locally and is not transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Expensely does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children’s Privacy

Expensely is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
