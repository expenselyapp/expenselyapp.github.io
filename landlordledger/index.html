Landlord Ledger

Track rental property receipts, fixed expenses, mileage trips, units, and property-first reports in one private iPhone app.

App Store

Built for small landlords

Landlord Ledger is focused on rental property bookkeeping: receipts, repeating property costs, business mileage, rental units, and exports organized by property.

Properties

Track Each Rental

Create rental properties, store addresses and notes, then attach receipts, fixed expenses, mileage trips, and units to the right property.

Receipts

Scan and Import

Capture paper receipts, import photos, or add PDFs. On-device OCR helps read merchant, amount, date, and searchable receipt text.

Fixed Costs

Monthly and Yearly Expenses

Add mortgage interest, insurance, municipal tax, school tax, condo fees, management fees, and other recurring property costs.

Mileage

Business Trip Tracking

Record trip date, purpose, origin, destination, distance, rate, linked property, and related receipts for landlord business travel.

Units

Rental Unit Details

Store unit number, monthly rent, start date, optional end date, and notes so property records include rent context.

Reports

Property-First CSV Export

Review dashboard totals by date range and export CSV reports ordered by rental property, then by date, with category and property totals.

Private by design

Landlord Ledger stores receipts, properties, trips, units, fixed expenses, and reports locally on your device. No cloud account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking SDK is required.
